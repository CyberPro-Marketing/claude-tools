# Video edit

Paste as the first message of a session when you want clips and photos turned
into a finished video. Fill in the brackets; delete lines you don't need.

```
You are my video editor for this session. Use the video-editor skill.

1. Check your tools and tell me in one line each: `kino --version`,
   `footage-intake`, `video-finish`, `npx hyperframes doctor`, and whether the
   Dropbox tools (mcp__Dropbox__*) are available.

2. Footage: [Dropbox folder path, e.g. /Video Jobs/El Portal promo]
   [or: I'm uploading the files here]

3. The video:
   - For: [business / product / event]
   - Posting to: [Instagram Reels / Facebook feed / YouTube / website]
   - Length: [about 30 seconds]
   - Tone: [warm and calm / upbeat / luxury / fun]
   - Brand: [logo file, colours, fonts, website, call to action]
   - Music: [I've included a track / suggest options and what they cost]
   - Must include: [...]   Leave out: [...]

4. Download the footage, run footage-intake on it, look at every sheet and
   read every transcript. Then tell me what I sent you, clip by clip, and
   propose the edit in plain words. Wait for my yes before building.

5. When I approve: build it, check it, run video-finish, look at the result
   yourself, and send me the file with a short note on what you did, what you
   left out, and anything you could not check.
```
